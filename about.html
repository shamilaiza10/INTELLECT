Why we exist

Learning shouldn't come with a price tag.

We started INTELLECT to make quality learning material available to anyone, regardless of background or budget. From technology and cyber security to conservation and biology, every topic on the site is written to be clear, useful, and easy to explore.

Whether you're a student working on a project or just curious about how something works, INTELLECT is built to give you a straightforward starting point — free of clutter, and free of cost.

Questions, answered.

Quick details about using INTELLECT and finding the right resource.

Ask us a question
Is INTELLECT free to use?

Yes. The learning resources are free to read, and you do not need to create an account to browse the site.

What subjects can I learn about?

The library covers technology, science, environment, digital safety, health awareness, and related topics. Browse the contents to see the current collection.

Can I suggest a topic or report a correction?

Yes. Send the team a message through the support page with your topic suggestion or the page details that need attention.

How can I support the project?

You can share the resources, send feedback, volunteer ideas, or contact the team through the support page.
